## Recommended 3-keyword answer from the case

When the user wanted 3 terms and considered `ai music` too difficult, the recommended set became:
1. ki musikgenerator
2. ai music generator
3. musik erstellen

### Why these 3

#### ki musikgenerator
- self rank around 15
- title directly supports it
- best balance of relevance + existing coverage + manageable difficulty
- best first push term

#### musik erstellen
- self rank around 62
- strong German functional intent
- easier than very broad English head terms
- good second push term

#### ai music generator
- self rank around 78
- high business value
- still worth buying and supporting with ASO
- but harder than the two terms above

## Difficulty framing used in the case

Do not claim exact required download counts.
Instead explain:
- already covered or not
- current rank depth
- likely relative difficulty
- recommended push order

Suggested wording:
- low / medium / medium-high / high
- easiest to push first
- requires more sustained search downloads
- broad term, larger volume needed
